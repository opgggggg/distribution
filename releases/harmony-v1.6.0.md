# CubeOffice 1.6.0 · HarmonyOS

## 中文

1. 电脑和 2in1 设备支持原生文档标签页，首页新增创建下拉菜单，平板支持模板选择。
2. 全面改进 Word、表格和演示文稿的编辑、排版与兼容性，并修复 AI 活动面板干扰助手命令的问题。

## English

1. Native document tabs on computers and 2-in-1 devices, a new creation dropdown on the home screen, and a template picker on tablets.
2. Broad improvements to Word, spreadsheet, and presentation editing, layout, and compatibility, plus a fix for the AI activity panel interfering with assistant commands.
