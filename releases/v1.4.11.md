# CubeOffice 1.4.11

## 中文

- 保存文档时保持当前编辑位置和导航状态，改善大型文档的编辑体验。
- 修复混合字体文本的光标定位，改进脚注和尾注的编辑、分页与搜索。
- 新建文档时可选择 QP 模板，AI 也可以查找模板并据此创建文档。
- 改进目录控件和分节页码的保存，修复关闭标签后的残留显示。
- 升级后自动刷新已配置的 AI 工具连接。

## English

- Keep your editing position and navigation state while saving, improving the experience with large documents.
- Fixed caret positioning in mixed-font text and improved footnote and endnote editing, pagination, and search.
- Choose QP templates for new documents, and let AI find templates and create documents from them.
- Improved saving of table-of-contents controls and section page numbering, and fixed lingering content after closing tabs.
- Automatically refresh configured AI tool connections after upgrades.
