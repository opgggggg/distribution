# CubeOffice 1.4.10

## 中文

- 新建演示文稿时可选择 AlphaLife 模板。
- 改进文档标签的打开、切换和拖动行为，切换标签时保持编辑器布局稳定。
- 改进最近文件预览，修复鼠标悬停时幻灯片缩略图抖动的问题。
- 优化文件提醒和 AI 活动区域布局，修复通过 CLI 恢复新建演示文稿后新增幻灯片失败的问题。

## English

- Choose the AlphaLife template when creating a new presentation.
- Improved document tab opening, switching, and dragging while keeping editor layouts stable.
- Improved recent-file previews and fixed slide thumbnails shifting on hover.
- Refined file alerts and the AI activity layout, and fixed adding slides after resuming a new presentation through the CLI.
