# CubeOffice 1.5.1 · HarmonyOS

## 中文

1. 电脑和 2in1 设备支持原生文档标签页：可拖出成为新窗口、拖回合并，切换时保留编辑状态。
2. 修复演示文稿放映切换幻灯片时背景来不及填充的问题。
3. 文档排版与图片改进：Word 跨页段落的行填充、不间断空格、按字体计算行高、跨页表格保持原有列宽，以及 PDF 图片提取与扫描件内嵌图片解码。

## English

1. Computers and 2-in-1 devices get native document tabs: drag one out into its own window, drag it back to merge, and switch without losing editor state.
2. Fixed the slide background not being filled before a slideshow transition.
3. Layout and image improvements: page-spanning paragraphs fill their last line, no-break spaces stay together, per-font line boxes, split tables keep their authored column widths, and PDF image extraction and scanned-image decoding are fixed.
