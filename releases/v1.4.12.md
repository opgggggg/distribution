# CubeOffice 1.4.12

## 中文

- 改进 PDF 原始图像提取、图像分页和保存文件名同步，并增加脚本叠加层预览选项。
- 修复 Word 标题、域和批注操作中的光标定位，编辑文字时保持内容控件完整。
- 修复演示文稿导入后的元素保留问题。
- Word 模板使用纵向预览，取消关闭文档时正确收起关闭提示。

## English

- Improved native PDF image extraction, image pagination, and saved filename synchronization, and added a script overlay preview option.
- Fixed caret positioning around Word headings, fields, and comments, and preserved content controls during text editing.
- Fixed preservation of imported presentation elements.
- Word templates now use portrait previews, and cancelling document closure dismisses the close prompt correctly.
