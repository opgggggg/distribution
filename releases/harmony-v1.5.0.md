# CubeOffice 1.5.0 · HarmonyOS

## 中文

1. 电脑和 2in1 设备支持原生文档标签页：可拖出成为新窗口、拖回合并，切换时保留编辑状态。
2. 修复演示文稿放映切换幻灯片时背景来不及填充的问题。
3. 同步文档改进：Word 排版更接近 Word（按字体计算行高、跨页表格保持原有列宽），PDF 扫描件内嵌图片可正确解码。

## English

1. Computers and 2-in-1 devices get native document tabs: drag one out into its own window, drag it back to merge, and switch without losing editor state.
2. Fixed the slide background not being filled before a slideshow transition.
3. Document improvements: Word layout matches Word more closely, with per-font line boxes and split tables that keep their authored column widths, and embedded images in scanned PDFs decode correctly.
