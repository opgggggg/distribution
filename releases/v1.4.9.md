# CubeOffice 1.4.9

## 中文

修复部分窗口标题、AI 连接页面和提示文字显示旧名称的问题，统一显示为 CubeOffice，并保留已有 AI 连接配置。

修复 macOS 切换或关闭文档窗口后，“关于”“系统设置”“检查更新”等菜单点击无反应的问题。

## English

Fixed outdated names in window titles, AI connection screens, and messages. These now consistently display CubeOffice while preserving existing AI connection settings.

Fixed unresponsive About, Settings, Check for Updates, and other menus after switching or closing document windows on macOS.
