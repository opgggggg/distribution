# CubeOffice 1.4.2

- 打开文档时自动下载缺失字体，已下载字体可离线使用。
- 桌面版统一意见反馈入口和表单，可在设置或关于中提交问题与建议；应用错误会上报脱敏诊断信息，不附带文档。
- 改善反馈类型下拉菜单的选择和键盘操作体验。

## English

- Missing fonts are downloaded when needed; downloaded fonts remain available offline.
- Desktop feedback now uses a consistent form accessible from Settings and About. App errors send redacted diagnostics without attaching documents.
- Improved feedback category selection and keyboard interaction.
